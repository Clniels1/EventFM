AI-powered search Let's you describe the vibe you're after How does this work?
EventFM uses an AI language model (sentence-transformers) to understand the meaning of your search, then compares it with every festival's title, genre, location and description. Festivals with similar meaning appear even if they don't contain your exact words. For example, "mellow tunes" can find jazz and acoustic events. Each result shows an AI match % for how closely it matches, and a Keyword match tag when your exact words appear too. Only the closest matches are shown.
